Academic and career focus

Computer technologies and cybersecurity

My studies focus on computer technologies and cybersecurity. I am especially interested in network security, ethical hacking, and practical ways to help users reduce common risks.

Why I built CyberReady

A practical starting point for everyday users

Many people understand that cybersecurity matters but do not know how to evaluate their current practices. CyberReady translates common safeguards into understandable questions, then gives users a score and specific actions they can review at their own pace.

Project responsibilities

From research to working prototype

I developed the project concept, researched user and hardware requirements, designed the interface, created the assessment logic, prepared the budget, and wrote the supporting documentation.
